## 💎 2. Core Non-Negotiable Invariants

Every change, suggestion, and line of code must strictly uphold these four founding pillars:

### I. Visual Dominance over Text ("Visual First, Details on Demand")
- **The 3–5 Seconds Rule:** Visual perception dominates text. Visitors form their judgment within 3–5 seconds based on layout geometry, company logos, badges, typography scale, and metrics.
- If an achievement or capability is not obvious visually at a glance, **for 90% of visitors it does not exist**.
- Long-form prose is strictly a secondary tier (*details on demand*), available in modals, drawers, or dedicated project routes.

### II. The Principle of Project Parity ("All Projects Are Paramount")
- **No Second-Class Projects:** Never divide work into "important commercial projects" vs. "minor pet/side projects".
- Whether a streaming giant with millions of users (**MEGOGO**), an enterprise mobile architecture (**CHI Software**), an indie bionic medical portal (**ESMEE**), an algorithmic C++20 compression patent (**LibPolyCodec**), an IoT telemetry network (**LoRa & ESP32**), an indie product with 10k+ downloads (**DᎥᗰᗩᒪᎥᑎᗩ**), or a scientific state grant/competition — **every project receives identical architectural respect, craft, and senior dignity**.
- Prospective clients of any scale must see: *Whatever problem I bring to Dmytro, it will be treated with highest priority, deep technical care, and uncompromising quality.*

### III. Versatile Multi-Disciplinary Spectrum
- Do not pigeonhole Dmytro as exclusively a mobile developer.
- Showcase equal, first-class competence across all seven capability domains:
  1. **Native Android** (high load, streaming, system integrations)
  2. **Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP)** (shared multiplatform logic)
  3. **Flutter & Dart** (cross-platform client applications)
  4. **Modern Web & Edge** (Astro Islands, Next.js, sub-second LCP, zero CLS)

  6. **IoT & Telemetry** (ESP32, LoRaWAN, MQTT, microcontrollers)

- **Never "dumb down" the portfolio.** Never remove technical depth, concrete dates, metrics, or technologies just to follow empty minimalist fads.
- When improving aesthetics, the objective is: **"Look sleeker, more modern, and cooler, while being at least as informative — and ideally even more informative through better visualization."**
